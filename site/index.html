From "they have my data" to proof

  1. Scan. Welcome, verify, password-reset and receipt emails reveal every account you hold and when you opened it.
  2. Flag. Breaches that happened after you joined, from the public Have I Been Pwned catalogue, matched on your device.
  3. Ask. A fixed legal letter (erase my data, or stop marketing and keep my account) opens in Gmail, addressed to the company's privacy team. You press Send.
  4. Prove. A company that confirms and then stays quiet for 30 days is marked Erased. One that says "deleted" and then emails you a promotion is marked Not erased, with the complaint pack ready.
Erased reads your mail on your device with Gemini Nano through Firebase AI Logic. Breach data: Have I Been Pwned. Apache-2.0.
