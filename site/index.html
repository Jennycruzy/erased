    The right to be deleted is the law. Getting it done isn't easy.

    GDPR, UK GDPR, California's CCPA, Canada's PIPEDA and Nigeria's NDPA all say a company must erase your data when you ask. Almost nobody does: when 32 European regulators reviewed 764 organisations in 2025, most "had not received a single request for erasure in the last two years" (EDPB, Feb 2026, p. 11). Three things get in the way.

    How Erased fixes it

    Five steps. The private ones run on your device; only public knowledge comes from the cloud. The model reads, plain rules decide.
