Try it on your own inbox

  1. Use Chrome 139+ on a desktop that can run Gemini Nano (a GPU with more than 4 GB of memory, or 16 GB RAM and 4 cores; 22 GB free disk).
  2. Download the extension, open chrome://extensions, switch on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked and pick the folder.
  3. Open Gmail, click the Erased icon, press Get on-device AI ready once, then Scan my inbox. Your number appears as the scan runs.
  4. Open Clean up to see companies breached after you joined and ones you no longer use, each with the route from the directory.

Your emails are read in your browser by the on-device model. The only things that leave are: the name of a company you ask Erased to look up, and, if you switch it on, anonymous outcome counts for the board.

Erased reads your mail on your device with Gemini Nano through Firebase AI Logic. Breach data: Have I Been Pwned. Apache-2.0.
